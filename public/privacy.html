Kuberan Dharma App (com.kuberansilks.dharma)

Privacy Policy & DPDP Act 2023 Notice

Effective Date: November 2026

1. Statutory Framework & Zero Personal Data Collection

This Privacy Notice complies with India’s Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023, the DPDP Rules, 2025, and global data minimization principles (GDPR). Kuberan Dharma is a local-first educational scripture app. We do not require registration, accounts, emails, phone numbers, location permissions, or personal identifiers. We do not collect, log, sell, or share any personal data or behavioral telemetry.

2. On-Device Local Storage & Retention

Your chosen reading language, daily reflection streak, and saved Stash bookmarks are stored strictly locally on your device (localStorage / Apple NSUserDefaults reason CA92.1; Android cloud backup disabled via android:allowBackup="false"). Data remains on-device solely until cleared via the in-app data reset/export controls or by uninstalling the app.

3. Background OTA Update Manifest Check

On native mobile builds when online, the app performs a read-only HTTPS GET check against https://jee1vk.github.io/kuberan-dharma-app/ota-manifest.json (hosted on GitHub Pages) to fetch SHA-256 integrity-verified scripture and UI bug-fix bundles. Updater telemetry is disabled (statsUrl: ""); zero personal data or usage telemetry is sent by the app, while standard HTTP delivery metadata is processed by GitHub Pages. In Airplane Mode, the app operates 100% offline.

4. Children’s Privacy (<18 Years — DPDP Act Sec 9)

Kuberan Dharma provides family-safe educational scripture content suitable for all ages. In compliance with Section 9 of the DPDP Act, 2023, we collect zero personal data from users under 18 years of age and perform zero behavioral monitoring, profiling, or targeted advertising.

5. No Ads & Open-Source Notices

Kuberan Dharma contains zero advertisements and zero third-party tracking SDKs. Open-source components used unmodified include: @capgo/capacitor-updater (MPL-2.0, source at github.com/Cap-go/capacitor-updater), Next.js & React (MIT), Ionic Capacitor (MIT), Lucide Icons (ISC), and MiniSearch (MIT).
