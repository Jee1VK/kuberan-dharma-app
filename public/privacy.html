Privacy Policy & Zero-Telemetry Pledge

Effective Date: November 2026

1. Zero Personal Data Collection

Kuberan Dharma is an offline-first educational scripture app. We do not require registration, accounts, emails, phone numbers, or personal details. We do not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data or usage telemetry with any server or third party.

2. On-Device Local Storage Only

Your chosen reading language, daily reflection streak, and saved Stash bookmarks are stored strictly locally on your device (localStorage / Apple NSUserDefaults reason CA92.1). This data never leaves your device.

3. No Ads & No Tracking SDKs

Kuberan Dharma contains zero advertisements, zero third-party analytics SDKs, and zero cross-app tracking.
